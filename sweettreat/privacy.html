Privacy
Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

The short version

SweetTreat doesn't collect, send or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What SweetTreat stores

To work, SweetTreat saves the following only on your device:

  • The spending you add (amount, optional note, date and time)
  • Your daily budget and Treat Jar balance
  • Basic app settings, such as when the last daily rollover happened

This information never leaves your device through SweetTreat. There are no SweetTreat servers, and I can't see your data.

What SweetTreat doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No third-party services or SDKs
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there's no data to share

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your SweetTreat data may be included in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple and your device settings, under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by SweetTreat.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time with Settings → Reset everything inside the app. Deleting SweetTreat from your device also removes all of its data.

Links

The Privacy button in the app opens this page in your web browser. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard technical information (like IP addresses) when you visit, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Children

SweetTreat doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
