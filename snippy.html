Snippy

A digital scrapbook for the web. Snip text, images and links from any page into personal scrapbooks you can come back to.

Waiting for Chrome Web Store approval

How it works

Snip

Right-click any text, image or link and choose "Snip to Snippy".

Collect

Clips land in scrapbooks with the page they came from, so you can always find the source.

Revisit

Browse your boards whenever you want. Delete anything with a tap.

Private by design

Your clips stay in your own browser. No accounts, no servers, no tracking. The only outside connection is the payment check if you choose the upgrade.

Read the privacy policy ↗

Free & Unlimited

Snippy is free to use. An optional one-time upgrade unlocks unlimited scrapbooks.
